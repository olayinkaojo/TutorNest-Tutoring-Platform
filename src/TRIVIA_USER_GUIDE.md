## 👨‍👩‍👧 FOR PARENTS

### Setting Up Your Child's Trivia Access

When you create a student profile for your child, you'll select which subjects they should study. **This selection determines which trivia subjects your child can access.**

#### Step-by-Step:

1. **Login** to your parent account
2. Navigate to **Dashboard → Children** tab
3. Click **"Add Child"** button

4. **Fill in Details:**
   - First Name
   - Last Name  
   - Date of Birth
   - Grade Level (This determines question difficulty)
   - Learning Goals

5. **Select Subjects** (IMPORTANT):
   
   Choose the subjects your child needs to focus on:
   
   **For Primary Students (Years 1-6):**
   - ☑️ Mathematics
   - ☑️ English
   - ☑️ Sciences
   - ☑️ History
   - ☑️ Geography
   - ☑️ Art (Years 1-2)
   - ☑️ Music (Years 1-2)
   - ☑️ Physical Education (Years 1-2)

   **For Secondary Students (Years 7-9):**
   - ☑️ Mathematics
   - ☑️ English
   - ☑️ Sciences
   - ☑️ History
   - ☑️ Geography
   - ☑️ Computer Science (Year 8+)

   **For GCSE/A-Level (Years 10-12):**
   - ☑️ Mathematics
   - ☑️ English
   - ☑️ Sciences
   - ☑️ History
   - ☑️ Geography
   - ☑️ Economics
   - ☑️ Psychology
   - ☑️ Computer Science

6. Click **"Add Child"**

### What Happens Next?

✅ Your child will ONLY see trivia questions for the subjects you selected
✅ They cannot access subjects you didn't select
✅ This keeps them focused on their actual learning needs

### Changing Subjects Later

Need to adjust? No problem!

1. Go to **Children** tab
2. Click **"Edit"** on your child's profile
3. Add or remove subjects
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

Changes take effect immediately!

## 👦👧 FOR STUDENTS

### How to Play Trivia Challenge

#### Step 1: Access Trivia

1. Login to your student account
2. Go to **Trivia & Rewards** tab
3. You'll see:
   - 🎮 Trivia Challenge Game Card
   - 🏆 Your Stats
   - 📊 Leaderboard

#### Step 2: Start a Challenge

1. Click **"Start Trivia Challenge"** button
2. You'll see subjects available to you
3. Choose ONE subject to play

**Example:**
- Mathematics
- Sciences

(You only see subjects your parent selected!)

#### Step 3: Answer Questions

Each round has **5 questions** from your chosen subject.

**For each question:**
- ⏱️ You have **30 seconds** to answer
- 🤔 Read carefully
- ✅ Click your answer choice
- 💡 Instant feedback (Correct ✓ or Incorrect ✗)

**XP Rewards:**
- ⭐ Easy questions: 10-30 XP
- ⭐⭐ Medium questions: 15-35 XP
- ⭐⭐⭐ Hard questions: 20-50 XP

#### Step 4: See Your Results

After 5 questions:

📊 **Results Screen shows:**
- Your score (e.g., 4/5 correct)
- Total XP earned
- Your rank on the leaderboard
- Streak status

#### Step 5: Play Again or Switch

**Options:**
- **Play Again** - Same subject, new questions
- **Choose Subject** - Pick a different subject
- **View Leaderboard** - See how you rank

## 🎯 UNDERSTANDING THE SYSTEM

### Question Difficulty

Questions are marked as:
- **Easy** 🟢 - Basic recall, simple concepts
- **Medium** 🟡 - Application, understanding
- **Hard** 🔴 - Advanced reasoning, complex problems

### Scoring System

**Your score is based on:**
- Number of correct answers (e.g., 4/5 = 80%)
- Time remaining (bonus for quick answers)
- Question difficulty (harder = more XP)

### XP & Levels

**Trivia XP adds to your total XP** from:
- Tutoring sessions
- Assessments
- Other activities

**More XP = Higher Level = Better Rank!**

### Leaderboards

**How Rankings Work:**
- Separate leaderboard per grade level
- Ranked by Total XP from trivia
- Updated in real-time
- Top 50 students displayed

**Stats Shown:**
- Your username
- Total XP
- Games played
- Best score

### Streaks

**Daily Streak Bonus:**
- Play trivia on consecutive days
- Earn bonus XP for streaks
- 🔥 7-day streak = 50 bonus XP
- 🔥 30-day streak = 200 bonus XP

## 💡 TIPS & STRATEGIES

### For Students:

1. **Read Carefully** 📖
   - Don't rush!
   - Eliminate obviously wrong answers first

2. **Practice Regularly** 📅
   - Play daily to maintain streaks
   - Consistent practice = better retention

3. **Focus on Weak Subjects** 🎯
   - Check your stats
   - Practice subjects with lower scores

4. **Use Curriculum Resources** 📚
   - Review curriculum PDFs first
   - Then play trivia to test knowledge

5. **Time Management** ⏰
   - Use first 10 seconds to think
   - Make your best guess if unsure

6. **Learn from Mistakes** ✏️
   - Wrong answers show correct answer
   - Remember for next time!

### For Parents:

1. **Align with School Curriculum** 🏫
   - Select subjects matching current schoolwork
   - Update subjects when terms change

2. **Monitor Progress** 📈
   - Check stats weekly
   - Look for improvement trends
   - Celebrate achievements!

3. **Balance Subjects** ⚖️
   - Don't overwhelm with too many subjects
   - 2-3 core subjects recommended

4. **Use as Supplement** 📝
   - Trivia reinforces tutoring lessons
   - Not a replacement for actual study

5. **Encourage Consistency** 🌟
   - Set daily trivia time (10-15 mins)
   - Reward streak milestones

## 🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING

### Problem: Student sees no subjects

**Solution:**
- Parent needs to select subjects in child profile
- Go to Children → Edit → Select subjects → Save

### Problem: Questions too easy/hard

**Solution:**
- Check grade level setting
- Update to appropriate grade
- Questions auto-adjust to grade

### Problem: Timer not working

**Solution:**
- Refresh the page
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache

### Problem: XP not updating

**Solution:**
- Complete full trivia round (all 5 questions)
- Wait 5 seconds for sync
- Refresh gamification display

### Problem: Leaderboard not showing

**Solution:**
- Play at least 1 complete round
- Check you're viewing correct grade
- Wait 30 seconds and refresh

### Problem: Can't start trivia

**Solution:**
- Ensure student profile is complete
- Check internet connection
- Try different browser
- Contact support if persists

## ❓ FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: How many questions per trivia round?**
A: Each round has exactly 5 questions.

**Q: Can I pause a trivia round?**
A: No, once started you must complete all 5 questions. Each has a 30-second timer.

**Q: Are questions repeated?**
A: Questions are randomized from large pools (200+ per subject per grade). You may see repeats eventually, but rare in early plays.

**Q: Does trivia cost anything?**
A: No! Trivia Challenge is completely free for all students.

**Q: Can I play trivia on mobile?**
A: Yes! The interface is fully responsive and works on phones and tablets.

### Scoring & XP

**Q: How is XP calculated?**
A: XP = (Correct answers × Question difficulty multiplier) + Speed bonus

**Q: Does trivia XP count toward my main level?**
A: Yes! Trivia XP adds to your total XP from all activities.

**Q: What's the maximum XP per round?**
A: Maximum is 200 XP (5 hard questions × 40 XP, all correct, answered quickly).

**Q: Do I lose XP for wrong answers?**
A: No, you just don't gain XP for that question. No penalties!

### Leaderboards

**Q: Are leaderboards global?**
A: No, each grade level has its own leaderboard. You only compete with students in your grade.

**Q: Can I see my friends' scores?**
A: You can see them on the leaderboard if they're in the top 50 for your grade.

**Q: How often do leaderboards update?**
A: Real-time! Rankings update immediately after completing a round.

**Q: What if two students have same XP?**
A: Tie-breaker: Best score, then games played, then who achieved it first.

### Subjects & Questions

**Q: Who writes the questions?**
A: All questions are curriculum-aligned and written by education specialists.

**Q: Are questions updated?**
A: Yes, question pools are reviewed and updated quarterly.

**Q: Can I request specific topics?**
A: Contact support with suggestions! We continuously improve content.

**Q: What if I find an error?**
A: Report it immediately to support with question ID. We'll investigate and fix!

### Technical

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions recommended).

**Q: Do I need to install anything?**
A: No, trivia works directly in your web browser.

**Q: Can multiple students play simultaneously?**
A: Yes! The system supports hundreds of concurrent users.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption and never share personal data.
