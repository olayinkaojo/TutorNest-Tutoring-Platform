FAQ

Questions we hear often

Still not sure? Here are the things most families ask before signing up.

How does the AI matching actually work?
When you create a child profile, you tell us their grade level, the subjects they need help with, their learning style, your schedule, and your budget per session. Our Smart Match engine then scores every verified tutor in our database against those six dimensions and ranks them by overall fit. You see a percentage match score next to each tutor — the higher the score, the more aligned the tutor is with your child's specific needs.
Are tutors background-checked and verified?
Yes. Every tutor on TutorNest goes through a verification process before their profile is made visible to families. We verify academic credentials, teaching qualifications, and relevant experience. Tutors who pass verification receive a Verified badge on their profile. We take trust seriously — your child's safety and learning come first.
What subjects and exam types are covered?
TutorNest covers a wide range of subjects including Mathematics, English, Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Humanities, and more. We support exam preparations across multiple curricula — GCSE, A-Levels, SAT/ACT, AP exams, WAEC/SSCE, JAMB/UTME, NECO, Common Entrance, IB, and more. You can filter tutors by subject and exam type when browsing.
How do payments work? Is it safe?
All payments are processed securely through our integrated payment providers, supporting cards and bank transfers across Africa, the UK, the US and internationally. You pay for sessions directly within the platform. Tutors are not paid until a session is completed, which protects both parties. You'll receive an invoice and receipt for every transaction within your account.
What if I'm not happy with my tutor?
We want every match to be the right one. If a session doesn't meet your expectations, you can rate your tutor after each session, send feedback, or request a new match at any time. If you believe there was a serious issue, contact our support team at contact@tutornest.org and we'll work to resolve it quickly. Our goal is for every family to find a tutor they love.
Can I switch tutors if the match isn't working?
Absolutely. There is no lock-in. If the tutor you matched with isn't the right fit — for any reason — you can return to your match results and book with another tutor. Your child profile and match preferences are saved, so finding a replacement is quick. We'd rather you switch than stay with a tutor who isn't working for your child.
