Getting started

Up and running in minutes

No lengthy forms, no waiting. From sign-up to first session in just a few steps.

1
Create a profile
Sign up as a parent and add your child's subjects, grade level, learning goals, and availability. Takes about two minutes.
2
Get matched
Our Smart Match engine scores every verified tutor against your child's profile and surfaces the best fits — ranked and explained.
3
Book a session
Choose a tutor, pick a time slot, and pay securely. Your tutor receives a confirmation instantly and you're on the calendar.
4
Track progress
After every session, read the tutor's report, see what's improving, and adjust goals. Results you can actually measure.
