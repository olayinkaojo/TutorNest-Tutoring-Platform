6. Bookings and Cancellations

Sessions are confirmed upon successful payment processing. Both the tutor and the parent/student will receive confirmation notifications.

Cancellations by Parents/Students

  • Full refund: Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the scheduled session start time are eligible for a full refund.
  • 50% refund: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled session start time are eligible for a 50% refund. The remaining 50% is paid to the tutor in recognition of their time and preparation.
  • No refund: Cancellations made after a session has commenced are not eligible for a refund.

Cancellations by Tutors

Tutors are expected to honour all confirmed bookings. Cancellations by tutors made less than 12 hours before the scheduled session start time constitute a late cancellation and may result in platform penalties, including reduced search visibility, warnings, or account suspension for repeated occurrences. Affected families will receive a full refund.

No-Shows

If a student/parent fails to attend a session without cancelling, the session is considered a no-show and is non-refundable. Tutor no-shows will result in a full refund to the family and appropriate platform penalties.

Extenuating Circumstances

TutorNest may, at its sole discretion, grant refunds or waive cancellation penalties in genuine extenuating circumstances (such as medical emergencies). Contact contact@tutornest.org within 24 hours of the session with appropriate context.
